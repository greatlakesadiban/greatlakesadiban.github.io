## GLAS 10th Annual Workshop Program

The [Great Lakes Adiban Society](https://greatlakesadiban.github.io/about/) is holding its 10th Annual Workshop on **October 3--4, 2026** at Western Michigan University, Kalamazoo, MI.  Meeting location is **02101 Dunbar Hall**.

### Saturday, October 3

*Continental Breakfast*

**9:00 am. The Ethics and Symbols of Love and Family**

- Cameron Cross / Towards a Comparative Analysis of Chivalry
- Eva Kuras / Cross-Cultural Connections of Courtly Love and Materiality in the Global Medieval
Romance
- Glenn Ellis / The *Adab* of Sex and Marriage in Majlisī’s *Ḥilyat al-Mutaqqīn* (*The Adornment of
the Pious*)

*Short Break*

**11:30 am. Persians and Others in Persian Epics**

- Kaveh Hemmat / China in Amir Khosrow’s Alexander Epic, a Theory of Invention, and the
Reception History of Nezami’s Alexander Epic
- Thomas Parsa / Creating a Cosmopolitan Epic: The *Garshasp-nama*, Recentering a Peripheral
Tradition and Expansive Persianate Identity

*Catered Lunch*

**2:30 pm. Textual Traditions of the Islamicate East Across Linguistic Boundaries**

- Kellen Parker Van Dam / Improving Arabic-script Text Recognition Accuracy With Synthetic
Training Data
- Jens Inden / A Close Reading Textual Comparison: *Kalīla wa-Dimna* and *al-Ḥikma al-Khālida*

*Dinner at Roca (6:30 pm)*

### Sunday, October 4

*Continental Breakfast*

**9:00 am. Poetry in Dialogue with Other Disciplines in the First Centuries A.H.**

- Jennifer Tobkin / What Can Muḥammad b. Dāwūd’s "Man of Our Times" Poems Tell Us About Abbasid Historiography?
- Ammar Farra / Craftsmanship, Revelation, and Transmission in Early Arabic Literature
